# Week 05 · scene guide

What the instructor reads before teaching Week 5; there is no rehearsal (plan/00_START_HERE.txt).

Deck: `site/weeks/week-05.html`. Thirteen pages: the opening situation, recall and outcomes, the eight scenes, a page on what a returned booking result promises (after scene 4), principles and checks, handoff and sources. The opening page shows one line of Design A's code from each component and the requested change, the longest booking from 120 to 90 minutes (R-02, a temporary variant). Its first click ("Search the code for 120") marks the three matching lines; its second ("Which lines hold the rule?") shows that two matches are copies of the rule, one match is a different 120 (seconds before an email retry) and the Reminder's copy, written `2 * 60`, was missed by the search. Then the week's question and the four questions the lecture answers appear.

Controls: Right/Left arrows change the page; Down/Up arrows step the scene; `r` replays the selected condition; `Shift+R` resets to the baseline. Every scene opens at its baseline, paused, also through its deep link. No scene has Run; Step and Back always move one state. The results page reveals each request's answer with its own button, in order, then each of the four claims.
