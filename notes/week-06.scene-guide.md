# Week 06 · scene guide

What the instructor reads before teaching Week 6; there is no rehearsal (plan/00_START_HERE.txt).

Deck: `site/weeks/week-06.html`. Thirteen pages: the opening situation, recall and outcomes, the eight scenes, a page on what each answer tells a requester about the write (after scene 5), principles and checks, handoff and sources. The opening page shows Ari's booking screen for C101, 10:00 to 11:00, and the request path with the inside of Campus Rooms covered. Its first click ("Book C101") ends in "No answer: the request timed out" and a prediction (is C101 booked for Ari?); its second ("Look inside Campus Rooms") uncovers the path: the request reached Booking, B-001 is stored as Confirmed, the response was lost on the way back. Then the week's question and the five questions the lecture answers appear.

Controls: Right/Left arrows change the page; Down/Up arrows step the scene; `r` replays the selected condition; `Shift+R` resets to the baseline. Every scene opens at its baseline, paused, also through its deep link. No scene has Run; Step and Back always move one state. The answers page reveals each answer's meaning with its own button, in order, then the three names (timeout, failure, unknown outcome).
