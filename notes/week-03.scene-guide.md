What the instructor reads before teaching Week 3; there is no rehearsal (plan/00_START_HERE.txt).

Deck: `site/weeks/week-03.html`. Thirteen pages: the opening situation, recall and outcomes, the eight scenes, the five models with the notation of the week, principles and checks, handoff and sources. The opening page draws the component view after its first click ("Draw the whole system") and lists five questions about B-001, each routed to the scene whose model answers it, after its second.

Controls: Right/Left arrows change the page; Down/Up arrows step the scene; `r` replays the selected condition; `Shift+R` resets to the baseline. Every scene opens at its baseline, paused, also through its deep link. In scene 8 the four view buttons (Context, Sequence, State, Data) open the state that shows that view; they are an alternative to Step under the baseline preset.
